We are excited to announce the release of the integration between OpenProject and XWiki . Together, both open source solutions provide organizations with an integrated platform for project management and enterprise knowledge management, making it easier to keep work and documentation connected while maintaining full control over your data. OpenProject 17.6 also introduces several improvements for agile planning with Backlogs, including sprint goals and a new all-sprints overview , as well as the ability to convert meeting agenda items directly into work packages .
