The best way to understand project management is to do it. That is the idea behind how the Vocational School Gutjahr in Halle (Saale) trains the next generation of IT specialists. As a school and IT competence center educating around 300 students in a three-year dual program, Gutjahr uses OpenProject to bring project methodologies to life.
