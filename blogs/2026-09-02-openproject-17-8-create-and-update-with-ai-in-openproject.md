OpenProject 17.8 takes the next step in connecting AI assistants with your project work. The MCP Server introduced in OpenProject 17.2 can now create and update work packages, add comments, and manage work package relations – while respecting the permissions and business rules configured in OpenProject. With this release, we are expanding the possibilities for AI-assisted workflows while keeping users in control.
