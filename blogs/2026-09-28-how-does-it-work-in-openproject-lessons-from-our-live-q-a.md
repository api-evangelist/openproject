In our recent live OpenProject demo and Q&A, you asked us about everything from resource and portfolio management to migration, integrations, wikis and permissions. We were happy to see that we could answer most questions positively. But the session also highlighted something equally important: sometimes OpenProject solves a familiar use case differently, and sometimes the answer is simply “not yet”.
