With OpenProject 17.9, we are making another useful feature available to everyone: Date alerts are now part of the free OpenProject Community edition. 🎉 Date alerts were introduced with OpenProject 12.4 as an Enterprise add-on. Almost four years later, we are happy to make them available to the whole OpenProject Community.
