We are excited to announce the release of OpenProject 17.7. This version introduces the new Resource management module (Enterprise add-on), enabling organizations to plan resources, allocate work, and manage capacity directly in OpenProject. Combined with new organizational capabilities available on all OpenProject plans, these features help organizations make better planning decisions based on people’s availability, skills, and working hours.
