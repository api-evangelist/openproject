It’s Passion Time at OpenProject! For two weeks, we put our regular work aside and make room for something different: ideas that people on our team have wanted to explore, improve or build – but that don’t necessarily find a place on our regular roadmap. This could be a feature we deeply miss in OpenProject, a performance improvement, one of those small changes that could make everyday work easier – or something beyond the product itself, such as an improvement to our website or an internal process.
