We are very excited to share some great news: bitcrowd is joining forces with OpenProject. From October, the experienced Berlin-based software team will work closely with us to strengthen OpenProject, support organizations on their path towards digital sovereignty and help us meet the growing demand we are seeing for open source alternatives to proprietary software. For us, this is much more than adding development capacity.
