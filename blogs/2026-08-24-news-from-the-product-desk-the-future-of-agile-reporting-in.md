High-performing agile teams do not just deliver work, they reflect on it. That habit of stopping after every sprint to ask what went well, what did not, and what should we change is the heartbeat of continuous improvement, or kaizen: small, steady adjustments that compound sprint after sprint. But a good retrospective needs good evidence, not just gut feeling.
